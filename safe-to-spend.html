How this works (the one rule)

A card purchase is the real spending event — the moment you log it, it raises that card's "Unfunded" amount on the Cards tab (money you now owe but haven't set cash aside for), but no cash leaves checking yet. The later card payment is just a transfer of cash from checking to the card — never a second expense. Only rows that actually move cash in or out of checking drive the projection and the headline number, so nothing is ever counted twice.

Safe to spend = spendable checking − your cash reserve − everything scheduled to leave checking before the next payday (+ anything scheduled to arrive before then). Moderate adds the next paycheck; Aggressive adds savings backup. A card purchase only moves this headline number once it's actually scheduled to leave checking — reconcile the card (or wait for its statement payment) to fold it in.

A Pending item that's still sitting there weeks after its date is usually forgotten cruft (an autopay that actually posted, or leftover sample data) — it keeps counting against you forever until you clear or delete it, so the app flags anything over two weeks stale.

Credit cards

CardBalanceNew since statementStatementDueAutopayFundedUnfunded

Balance and Funded are quick edits — click the number and type. New since statement is anything charged or refunded on the card since your last reconcile; it's automatic, folded straight into Unfunded (owed − funded) so a new purchase shows up here immediately. When a statement arrives, hit Reconcile: it prefills the live total, and saving schedules the payment for you (full / minimum / custom, per the card's autopay) and resets "new since statement" to zero.
